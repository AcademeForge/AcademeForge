About Us

How it started

AcademeForge began in January 2024 as a Telegram community where students solved doubts, joined quizzes, shared study resources, and learned together in a supportive environment.

How it's going

Today it is a student-first learning ecosystem with practical courses, AI guidance, quizzes, certifications, productivity tools, and a growing community to help every learner succeed.
