1. Information We Collect

At AcademeForge, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our digital platforms.

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and educational details provided during registration.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our platforms (e.g., learning heatmaps, course progress).
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, and device identifiers to optimize your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our educational services.
  • To track your learning progress and issue certificates.
  • To personalize your experience using AcademeForge AI.
  • To communicate important updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard encryption and secure modern hosting to protect your data. Your personal profile details are heavily encrypted. We do not sell your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data. You can instantly request account deletion from inside the AF Nexus app settings, or by emailing privacy@academeforge.in. If you log back in within 90 days, the request is canceled. Otherwise, all associated data is permanently wiped.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@academeforge.in.
