Frequently Asked Questions

General & Accounts
AcademeForge is a global all-in-one learning ecosystem built for students. We provide text-based Explore courses, an AI mentor (AF ProGuide), gamified quizzes, the Scholars Test portal, and free productivity apps like Timezy and Capacity.
No. A single AcademeForge account grants you access to all our platforms seamlessly, including AF Nexus, AcademeForge AI, Scholars Test, and Timezy.
Many of our tools (like Timezy, Capacity, and our brain games) are completely free. Premium certification courses and specialized practice tests are offered at highly affordable pricing for students.
You can reach out to our team directly via the Contact Us page, or email us at help@academeforge.in.
Courses & Certificates
Explore courses are text-based, read-and-learn modules. This means you can learn at your own pace without needing high bandwidth for videos. Once you complete the reading and assessments, you earn your certificate instantly.
Every certificate issued by AcademeForge comes with a unique Certificate ID. You (or an employer) can visit the Certificate Verification Portal to instantly authenticate it.
Platforms & Exams
AST is our official examination platform for school students. Students receive an official admit card with a Roll Number and take secure, standardized assessments at ast.academeforge.in.
Our AI (AF ProGuide) acts as a personal mentor. It can assist with coding logic, explain difficult concepts, help you brainstorm, and guide you through your course material.
Timezy is a productivity and time management app for visual task scheduling. Capacity is a private wellbeing platform for mood tracking and mindfulness. Both are free companion apps in the ecosystem.
Internships & Onboarding
You can apply by visiting the Internship Apply page. Select your desired role and submit the form. Our remote internships provide live workspace experience and professional training.
You can track your application in real-time by visiting the Application Status page using the email or phone number you applied with.
You can confirm any active member's identity using the Team Verification Portal. Enter their Member ID to securely retrieve their official status and role.
