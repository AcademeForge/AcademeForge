Help Center & Guides

Everything you need to navigate the AcademeForge ecosystem. Let's get you started on the right path.

Platform Navigation & UI Guide
You can find the universal Search button (magnifying glass icon) at the top right corner of the header on desktop. On mobile, it is also easily accessible in the top right. Clicking it opens the Command Palette.
You can change your theme preferences by scrolling to the very bottom footer of any page. If you are on a mobile device, open the Mobile Menu (hamburger icon) and you will find the Theme toggle buttons (sun, moon, and system icons) at the bottom.
Look at the main header at the top of the page. Click or hover over the 'Products' dropdown menu to see the complete list of platforms like AF Nexus, AcademeForge AI, Timezy, and more.
The 'Download Center' button is highlighted in the top right corner of the main navigation bar on desktop. You can also find a direct link in the footer under 'Resources'.
Language toggles (EN / HI) are located in the top right corner of the desktop navigation bar next to the search button. On mobile, open the drawer menu and scroll down to the 'Language' section.
In the top header menu, click on the 'Internship' dropdown and select 'Application Status'. Enter your registered email or phone number to see real-time updates.
Click on the 'Other tools' dropdown in the main top header. From there, you can easily access the Certificate Verification Portal and the Team Verification Portal.
If you are on a smartphone or small screen, tap the hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines) in the top right corner. This will slide open a drawer containing all links, products, and settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

About AcademeForge & History
AcademeForge officially began in January 2024 as a humble Telegram community. It started as a small group where students gathered to solve doubts, participate in quizzes, and share study resources.
It was born out of a simple need for a supportive student environment. After the success of the initial Telegram community, the founders realized the massive demand for accessible, high-quality educational tools, leading to the development of the current unified digital ecosystem.
AcademeForge was founded by a passionate team of student-first educators and technologists dedicated to democratizing learning. It is built by students, for students.
Our mission is 'Learning Beyond The Classroom.' We aim to provide an all-in-one ecosystem that not only focuses on academics but also on mental wellbeing, digital skills, and productivity.
Yes, AcademeForge operates as a recognized digital platform focusing on educational technology and professional student development.
General & Accounts
Today, AcademeForge is a global all-in-one learning ecosystem. We provide text-based Explore courses, an AI mentor, gamified quizzes, official examinations, and free productivity apps like Timezy and Capacity.

Many of our core tools, including Timezy, Capacity, and our brain games, are 100% free. Premium certification courses and specialized practice tests are offered at highly affordable, student-friendly pricing.
You can reach our support team directly via the Contact Us page, or by emailing us at help@academeforge.in. We typically respond within 24 hours.
Yes, student privacy is our top priority. We use secure modern hosting and encrypt all personal profile details. We do not sell your data to third parties.
You can request account deletion at any time by emailing privacy@academeforge.in from your registered email address. All associated data will be permanently wiped within 7 days.
Go to the login page of any AF platform and click 'Forgot Password'. A secure reset link will be sent to your registered email address.
Courses & Certificates
Explore courses are highly optimized, text-based, read-and-learn modules. This allows you to learn at your own pace without needing high-bandwidth internet for heavy videos. Complete the readings and assessments to earn your certificate instantly.
Yes, every certificate issued by AcademeForge comes with a unique Certificate ID and QR code. They are highly valued for demonstrating practical digital skills and can be showcased on LinkedIn or your resume.
Anyone can visit our Certificate Verification Portal on the website. By entering the unique Certificate ID, the system will instantly authenticate the document and display the student's credentials.
No, Explore courses are completely self-paced. You have lifetime access to the material once enrolled.
Yes, you can retake module assessments. We encourage learning from mistakes, so you can review the material and attempt the test again.
Platforms & Ecosystem
AF Nexus is the central hub of our ecosystem. It is where you track your learning progress, manage your enrolled courses, view your heatmap, and connect with the community.
AF ProGuide acts as your 24/7 personal AI mentor. It can assist with coding logic, explain difficult academic concepts, help you brainstorm ideas, and guide you through your course material.
AST is our official examination platform for school and college students. Students receive an official admit card with a Roll Number and take secure, standardized assessments at ast.academeforge.in.
Timezy is our productivity and time management app for visual task scheduling. Capacity is our private wellbeing platform for mood tracking and mindfulness. Both are completely free companion apps.
Zenopulsky is an independent publishing platform within our ecosystem focused on journalism, technology, education, society, and stories that matter to students.
Yes! We welcome student writers and independent journalists. You can submit your pitches through the Zenopulsky platform directly.
Nexora Studio is the creative and innovation wing behind the AcademeForge ecosystem. They focus on design, branding, product development, and creating beautiful digital experiences.
AF Bazzar is the official AcademeForge marketplace where you can purchase digital resources, study materials, and official physical merchandise.
We believe in productive breaks. Our games are integrated into the ecosystem to help you sharpen your focus and logic skills between heavy study sessions.
Technical & Mobile Support
Yes! While all our platforms are highly responsive web apps, we also offer an official Android APK that you can download directly from our Download Center.
Select features, like the Android app's downloaded courses and local Timezy scheduling, work offline. However, AI features and progress syncing require an active internet connection.
We support all modern browsers including Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox. For the best experience, keep your browser updated to the latest version.
Community & Social
You can join our active student communities on Telegram and Discord using the social links in our footer. It is the best place to find study partners and get instant help.
Yes. We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against bullying, hate speech, and spam. Our communities are strictly moderated to maintain a safe learning environment.
Team & Internships
Our platforms are built and maintained by a dedicated internal engineering team, alongside a vibrant community of interns and open-source contributors from across the country.
You can apply by visiting the Internship Apply page. Select your desired role (Engineering, Design, Marketing, etc.) and submit your form. Our remote internships provide live workspace experience.
We offer both! We have structured, experience-based unpaid learning internships for beginners to build their resume, as well as paid stipend roles for advanced developers and designers.
You can track your application in real-time by visiting the Application Status page. Just log in using the email or phone number you applied with to see your live timeline.
You can confirm any active member's identity using the Team Verification Portal. Enter their Member ID to securely retrieve their official status, role, and tenure with us.
